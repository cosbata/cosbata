## Selected work

### [LENS](https://github.com/cosbata/lens) · Live world briefings

An open-source briefing that explains what matters, where it is happening, and why it was selected—with context and sources rather than a stream of headlines.

`live briefings` · `news context` · `information design`

### [Parallel Research](https://github.com/cosbata/parallel-research) · Research that stays inspectable

A workspace that turns broad questions into distinct research lanes, keeping evidence, working judgments, and opposing views connected from brief to synthesis.

`research planning` · `provenance` · `human judgment`

### [CBAM](https://github.com/cosbata/cbam) · Compliance, grounded in evidence

Evidence-first ISO and supplier-compliance workflows for Korean and U.S. organizations: diagnose gaps, verify documents, and produce reviewable submissions.

`document automation` · `gap analysis` · `reviewable workflows`

### [slip](https://github.com/cosbata/slip) · Shared context for hard outcomes

An anonymous community for rejection, layoffs, and difficult fundraising stories—built to reveal patterns that private experiences otherwise hide.

`structured stories` · `moderation` · `collective learning`
